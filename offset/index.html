Information

Parsons Communication Design is thrilled to announce the Third Annual Offset Art Book Fair, hosted in partnership with Draw Down Books.

Offset facilitates the exhibition and vending of zines, books, and other print ephemera, supporting independent publishing & zine-making culture. The fair will be prefaced on Friday, October 23rd, 6–8pm by Process Pamphlets: Issues 6–9 Launch & Conversation in the Anna-Maria and Stephen Kellen Auditorium at Parsons. All events are free and open to the public.

Registration for the Offset Art Book Fair is free but requires a timed ticket entry (11am, 1pm and 2:30pm). Advance ticket registration is required due to limited capacity in the building. External visitors to The New School must register in advance. For more information contact offsetartbookfair@gmail.com.
